Reference Only: Based on Dhan's published fee structure as of 2026 (dhan.co/pricing). Actual charges may vary.
Equity Delivery: ₹0 brokerage for all traders (male & female). STT 0.1% both sides. DP ₹12.5+GST per ISIN.
Equity Intraday: ₹20 or 0.03% (lower) per order for male. Female traders get 50% off — ₹10 per order.
Futures: ₹20 or 0.03% (lower) per order. Female: ₹10. STT 0.05% sell side (Budget 2026, eff. 1 Apr 2026). Exchange 0.00183%.
Options: ₹20 flat per order. Female: ₹10 flat. STT 0.15% on sell premium (Budget 2026, eff. 1 Apr 2026). Exchange 0.03553%.
CTT (MCX): 0.01% on sell side for commodity futures; 0.05% for commodity options on MCX.
DP Charges: ₹12.50 per ISIN + GST charged by CDSL on delivery sell. Dhan does not add extra DP fee.
GST & SEBI: GST 18% on brokerage + exchange + SEBI charges. SEBI ₹10 per crore. Verify at dhan.co/pricing.
