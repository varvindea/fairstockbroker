01 · Fundamentals

What Is Term Insurance?

A term plan is pure life insurance — it pays the full sum assured to your nominee if you die within the chosen policy term. No investment, no maturity payout for survivors. Just maximum protection at the lowest possible cost.

Term insurance is the most cost-efficient form of life cover available in India. A 30-year-old non-smoking male can secure ₹1 crore of coverage for roughly ₹550–₹750 per month — less than a single restaurant meal. The nominee receives the entire sum assured as a tax-free lump sum (or structured income, depending on the payout option chosen) if the life insured passes away during the policy term.

02 · Landmark Reforms

The Reforms That Changed Everything (2025–26)

Three seismic changes in 2025 have permanently altered how term insurance is priced, sold, and regulated in India. Every buyer should understand them.

September 22, 2025
GST Removed on Individual Life & Health Insurance Premiums
Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman's landmark announcement eliminated the 18% GST on individual term and health insurance premiums. This instantly lowered effective premium costs for all policyholders. Industry data shows a 20% jump in new business premiums in October 2025 and a 24% surge in November 2025 — directly attributable to this reform. Note: group insurance and unit-linked plans retain GST at 18%.
December 2025
Sabka Bima Sabki Raksha Act — 100% FDI Opens
The Lok Sabha and Rajya Sabha passed the Sabka Bima Sabki Raksha (Amendment of Insurance Laws) Bill, 2025, raising the FDI cap in insurance from 74% to 100% under the automatic route. Effective February 9, 2026, via Press Note No. 1 of 2026. This is expected to attract global expertise, intensify competition, reduce premiums further, and accelerate digital innovation. FDI cap history: 26% (1999) → 49% (2015) → 74% (2021) → 100% (2026).
April 1, 2026
Risk-Based Capital (RBC) Norms Replace Solvency Ratio Benchmarks
IRDAI moved from a flat 1.5× solvency ratio to Risk-Based Capital norms — insurers must now hold capital proportional to their specific risk exposure. Diverse, higher-risk portfolios must hold more capital. This is expected to make financially robust insurers more competitive and may cause marginal premium adjustments at smaller players.
April 1, 2026
Insurance Fraud Monitoring Framework 2025 Goes Live
The IRDAI's new fraud monitoring guidelines (issued October 9, 2025) repeal the 2013 framework. New requirements: dedicated Fraud Monitoring Committee, standardised reporting, mandatory cybersecurity framework, and coverage extended to all distribution channels. This adds a layer of protection against fake claims that previously eroded industry trust.
Ongoing 2026
Bima Sugam — India's "UPI for Insurance"
Bima Sugam is an IRDAI-backed zero-commission online insurance marketplace. Once fully operational, it is expected to strip out agent commissions entirely, potentially reducing premiums by 15–20% further for online buyers. The platform also acts as a Public Insurance Registry — a digital locker linking all policies to one ID, making claims far easier for nominees to track and process.

The combined effect of GST removal and 100% FDI clears two historic bottlenecks for the industry. For consumers, this is the best time in a decade to buy term insurance.

CSR vs ASR — The Key Difference

A 99% CSR sounds perfect — but if the insurer only settles 80% of the claimed amount, your high-value ₹2 crore policy may not pay out fully. Always check the Amount Settlement Ratio (ASR) alongside CSR, particularly for large sum assured policies. Axis Max Life leads on ASR at 96.2% among high-volume private players.

06 · Riders & Add-Ons

Riders That Transform Your Cover

A base term plan protects against death. Riders extend that protection to critical illness, accidents, disability, and more — at a fraction of the cost of a separate policy.

  • 🏥
    Critical Illness Rider (CI) Pays a lump sum immediately on diagnosis of a covered illness — cancer, heart attack, stroke, kidney failure, etc. Leading plans cover 40–64 conditions. Crucially, this payment is in addition to the base death benefit (in most plans), giving the policyholder funds for treatment while still alive. For a 30-year-old, adding ₹10L CI cover to a ₹1Cr plan costs roughly 26% extra premium.
  • 🚗
    Accidental Death Benefit Rider (ADB) Pays an additional sum (on top of base cover) if death occurs due to an accident. Most plans offer 50–100% of base sum assured as the rider amount. Ideal for anyone who drives regularly, commutes by road, or works in a physically risky environment. Deaths due to heart attacks have increased 12.5% YoY (NCRB 2022), making this and CI complementary rather than substitutes.
  • 🩹
    Accidental Total & Permanent Disability Rider (ATPD) Pays a benefit (lump sum or income) if the policyholder suffers total permanent disability — e.g., loss of both limbs, eyesight, or paralysis — due to an accident. Unlike the death benefit, this pays while the person is alive, compensating for lost earning capacity. Often bundled with ADB at a marginal extra cost.
  • 🛡️
    Waiver of Premium (WOP) Rider If the policyholder suffers a critical illness, permanent disability, or (in some plans) any disability, all future premiums are waived while the policy continues in full force. This prevents a lapse exactly when the cover is most needed — during a medical crisis. Often available as a free in-built benefit in premium plans; otherwise available at minimal extra cost.
  • ⏳
    Terminal Illness Accelerator / Benefit If diagnosed with a terminal illness (typically with ≤12 months prognosis), the insurer pays 50–100% of the sum assured immediately. This allows the policyholder to meet medical costs, settle affairs, or support family while still alive. Tata AIA pays 50% on terminal illness diagnosis — one of the better implementations in 2026.
  • 💰
    Return of Premium (TROP) Add-On All premiums paid are returned if the policyholder outlives the policy term. Death benefit is still paid if the policyholder dies during the term. The catch: TROP plans cost 2–3× more than pure term plans. They are technically not investment vehicles but appeal to buyers who dislike "losing" premiums. With GST now removed, the cost differential has narrowed slightly.
  • 📈
    Life Stage Benefit / Increasing Cover Allows automatic or voluntary increase in sum assured at life milestones — marriage, birth of first child, home purchase. Kotak Life offers a 50% increase on marriage or home buying. Tata AIA and Axis Max Life have similar features. Smart Cover (Axis Max) gives 1.5× cover for the first 15 years when income and liabilities are highest, reverting to 1× thereafter.
  • 🏨
    Hospi-Cash / Hospital Daily Cash Rider Pays a fixed daily benefit (typically ₹1,000–₹5,000/day) for each day of hospitalisation, up to a maximum of 30–40 days per year. Covers incidental expenses not covered by health insurance — transport, food, loss-of-pay during hospitalisation. Bharti AXA Flexi Term Pro offers this as a distinct add-on.
2026 Rider Strategy

Most advisors recommend at minimum: (1) Critical Illness Rider if you have family history of lifestyle diseases, and (2) Waiver of Premium for continuity protection. The ADB rider is low-cost and high-value for commuters. Avoid stacking too many riders — the premium compounds and may exceed the cost of a dedicated standalone health policy.

Important

The premium deduction under Section 80C / Section 123 applies only in the old tax regime. Under the new simplified tax regime (default from FY 2024–25), the 80C-equivalent deduction is not available. Buyers under the new regime still benefit from a tax-free death benefit. Consult a CA for your specific situation.

TROP Tax Treatment Note

For Return of Premium plans, the returned premium amount is exempt under Section 10(10D) / Schedule II of ITA 2025 only if the premium does not exceed 10% of the sum assured (for policies issued after April 1, 2012). Most modern TROP plans are structured to comply with this condition. Always verify with the insurer before purchase.

09 · Buyer's Guide

How to Choose & Buy the Right Plan

A structured approach prevents the two most common mistakes: underinsuring (buying too little cover) and over-insuring with unsuitable riders that add cost without proportional benefit.

Step 1 — Calculate Your Cover Requirement

The industry rule of thumb is 10–20× your annual income. A more precise approach — the Human Life Value (HLV) method — considers your current income, expected income growth, years to retirement, existing liabilities (home loan, personal loan), dependents' needs, and existing investments / assets.

Quick Formula

Minimum Cover = (Annual Income × 10) + Outstanding Loans + Children's Education Fund − Existing Investments/Insurance

Example: Annual income ₹12L, home loan ₹40L, education fund needed ₹15L, existing savings ₹8L → Minimum cover ≈ ₹120L + ₹40L + ₹15L − ₹8L = ₹1.67 Crore. Round up to ₹2 Crore.

Step 2 — Check These Metrics Before Choosing an Insurer

  • CSR (Claim Settlement Ratio): Should be 98%+ consistently over 3 years, not just one year.
  • ASR (Amount Settlement Ratio): Should be 90%+ — especially critical for ₹2Cr+ policies.
  • Solvency Ratio: Should be well above 1.5× (IRDAI minimum). 2× and above gives comfort.
  • Complaint Volume: Below 5 complaints per 10,000 claims is excellent.
  • Claim Speed: Does the insurer offer fast-track claims (e.g., 24-hour or 4-hour settlement for eligible claims)?
  • Digital Infrastructure: Can you track claims, update nominees, and download documents via app?

Step 4 — NRI Buyers in 2026

NRIs can now purchase Indian term insurance via video / telemedical examination from their country of residence. The policy is issued in Indian Rupees. Premiums can be paid in foreign currency (subject to FEMA compliance). Death benefits are repatriable to the NRI's country of residence. The maximum entry age is 60 for most NRI plans.

Step 5 — How to File a Claim (for Nominees)

Step 1 — Intimate the Insurer
Report the policyholder's death as soon as possible. Obtain the Claim Intimation Form (available online or at branch). Fill in: policy number, insured's name, date of death, cause of death, nominee's ID.
Step 2 — Submit Documents
Death certificate (original + attested copies), policy document, claimant's identity proof, cancelled cheque / bank passbook (with account number, name, IFSC). For accidental death: FIR, post-mortem report. For illness-related death: hospital / medical records.
Step 3 — Track & Follow Up
Most insurers now assign a dedicated Claims Relationship Officer (CRO) the next working day. Track claim status via insurer's app. If no response within 30 days, file a complaint with IRDAI's Bima Bharosa portal.
Step 4 — Receive Payout
IRDAI mandates settlement within 30 days of receiving all documents. If investigation required, maximum 90 days total + 30 days after investigation close. Interest is payable on delayed claims under IRDAI regulations.
10 · Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions Answered

What is the minimum and maximum age to buy term insurance in 2026?

Minimum entry age is 18 years. The previous maximum of 65 years has been removed by IRDAI — insurers must offer at least one plan to applicants of any age. In practice, most competitive plans accept entry up to 60–65 years, with some accepting up to 70.

Is there any medical test required?

For most online plans with sum assured up to ₹50 lakh and applicants below 35 with no health issues, insurers may issue the policy without a physical medical test using telemedical examination (phone questionnaire) or based on declaration. Higher sum assured, older age, or health conditions typically require blood tests, ECG, and sometimes a physician examination.

What happens if I miss a premium payment?

Under IRDAI's 2026 grace period rules, you have a legally mandated buffer: 30 days for monthly premiums, 30 days for annual premiums from the due date. During this window, the policy remains in force. If you miss the grace period, the policy lapses. However, you can revive it within 2 years by paying outstanding premiums plus interest. Special revival campaigns (like those seen in early 2026) offer up to 30% waiver on late fees.

Can I have multiple term plans from different insurers?

Yes. There is no restriction on buying multiple term plans from different insurers. Each plan will independently pay out its sum assured to the nominee. You must disclose all existing insurance policies when applying for a new one. The combined cover is considered during underwriting for very high amounts.

Is suicide covered under term insurance?

No. If the policyholder dies by suicide within 12 months of purchasing or reviving the policy, the death benefit is not paid. After 12 months, the full death benefit is payable. This is a standard industry exclusion mandated by IRDAI.

Is the death benefit from a term plan taxable?

The death benefit received by the nominee is fully exempt from income tax under Section 10(10D) of the Income Tax Act 1961 / Section 11 of the Income Tax Act 2025, provided the annual premium does not exceed 10% of the sum assured (for policies issued after April 1, 2012). This exemption has no upper rupee limit.

What is Bima Sugam and when will it launch?

Bima Sugam is IRDAI's proposed unified online insurance marketplace — described as the "UPI of insurance." It aims to offer zero-commission term, health, and general insurance policies alongside a Public Insurance Registry where all policies are linked to a single policyholder ID. A soft launch is anticipated in 2026–27, with full commercial rollout expected in phases. This is expected to further reduce online premiums by 15–20%.

Will the 100% FDI change reduce my existing policy premium?

No. Premiums on existing policies are locked in at purchase. However, new policies bought after 2026 may benefit from increased competition as foreign insurers and joint ventures enter India, leading to more innovative products and potentially lower pricing — particularly for online channels.

What is the difference between CSR and ASR?

CSR (Claim Settlement Ratio by number) tells you what % of claims filed were approved. ASR (Amount Settlement Ratio) tells you what % of the total rupee value of claims was actually paid. An insurer with 99% CSR but 80% ASR may be approving many small claims while partially rejecting large ones. For high-value term plans (₹1 Cr+), ASR is equally or more important than CSR.

Final Recommendation for 2026

Buy term insurance as early as possible — premiums are cheapest when you are young and healthy. Post-GST removal, 2026 offers the most affordable premiums in India's insurance history. Combine a pure term plan with at least a Critical Illness rider and Waiver of Premium for comprehensive protection. Buy online, compare CSR + ASR + solvency + complaint volume, and choose a plan you'll hold for decades — not just the one with the lowest headline premium today.
