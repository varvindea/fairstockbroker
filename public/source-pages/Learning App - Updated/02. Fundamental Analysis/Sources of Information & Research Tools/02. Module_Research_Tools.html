03 — Exact Step-by-Step Workflows

What to Click, In What Order

These three workflows take you from opening the website to having actionable research insights — following the exact steps a professional analyst would use, simplified for any experience level.

📊
Screener.in — 60-Minute Deep Dive on Any Stock
Use this for every company you shortlist for potential investment
1
Go to screener.in → Type company name in search → Select "Standalone" or "Consolidated"
Always check both. Standalone = parent company only. Consolidated = including subsidiaries. For manufacturing companies, consolidated usually matters more — subsidiaries may have key assets or debt. Sun Pharma's US business (most profitable) only shows in consolidated.
screener.in/company/NESTLEIND/consolidated/
2
Check the "Top Ratios" section first — this is the 30-second quality check
Look at: Stock PE, Book Value, Return on Equity, Return on Capital Employed, Debt to Equity, and Dividend Yield. Compare to sector peers. If ROE is below 12% or D/E above 3×, you may want a clear reason before proceeding deeper. Write down any ratio that looks unusual — investigate in later steps.
3
Click "Profit & Loss" tab → Check 10-year Revenue, PAT, OPM% trend
You want: Revenue growing consistently. OPM% (operating margin) stable or improving. PAT growing faster than revenue (improving margins). Click the chart icon next to any metric to see a 10-year visual trend. A company with ₹500 Cr revenue 10 years ago and ₹5,000 Cr today has grown 10× — worth noting.
Look for: Revenue CAGR >12%, OPM >15%, PAT CAGR >15%
4
Click "Cash Flow" tab → Compute CFO/PAT ratio for last 5 years
Find "Cash from Operating Activity" (CFO) and "Net Profit" (PAT). Divide CFO by PAT for each year. Average above 0.85× = excellent quality. If any year shows CFO/PAT below 0.5×, read the notes to find the reason — CapEx cycle? WC expansion? One-time event? This is the most important 2-minute check on the Cash Flow tab.
Quality rule: 5-year avg CFO/PAT > 0.85× = strong; <0.6× = investigate
5
Check "Shareholding" section → Promoter holding trend + FII/DII changes
Promoter holding above 50% is standard. Decreasing promoter holding (quarter-on-quarter) is a warning signal — promoters may be exiting at high prices. Increasing institutional (FII + DII) holding often precedes re-rating. Pledge percentage above 20% of promoter holding is a risk factor — pledged shares can be sold by lenders.
6
Run a Custom Screen → Find similar quality companies for peer comparison
Go to Screener.in → Screens → Create New Screen. Use the formula builder to filter by sector + quality metrics. Example: Industry == Cables AND Return on equity > 15 AND Debt to equity < 0.5. This instantly shows all cable companies with ROE >15% and low debt — your peer group for comparison. Export to Excel for side-by-side analysis.
Free screen formula: Return on equity > 15 AND Debt to equity < 0.5 AND Sales > 500

Trendlyne — 20-Minute Stock Score & Ranking
Use this to rank your Screener shortlist and spot analyst consensus
1
Go to trendlyne.com → Search your stock → Open the stock dashboard
The Trendlyne dashboard shows live price, DVM score, Durability/Valuation/Momentum sub-scores, analyst consensus, and recent news — all on one page. This gives you a complete 60-second snapshot that would take 2 hours to compile manually from individual sources.
trendlyne.com/stocks/POLYCAB/POLYCAB/dashboard
2
Check the DVM Score → Interpret each component separately
Durability (D) >70 = business quality is high — consistent revenue growth, high ROCE, low debt. Valuation (V) >50 = not obviously overvalued relative to history. Momentum (M) >60 = price above key moving averages, EPS estimates being upgraded. Ideal: all three above 60. Caution: if D is high but V is 20 — great business but expensive.
Target: D >70, V >50, M >55 = strong setup for research
3
Click "Analyst" tab → Check consensus recommendation + target price
See how many analysts say Buy / Hold / Sell, and the average 12-month target price. If 15 analysts say Buy with an average target 30% above current price — that's strong institutional conviction. Also check: are estimates being revised upward (positive) or downward (negative) over the last 3 months? Estimate upgrades are the most powerful short-term catalyst for stock prices.
4
Use Pre-built Screens → Filter by quality + momentum without building formulas
Go to Trendlyne → Screener → Browse Screens. Useful pre-built screens: "High ROCE & ROE Consistency" (finds businesses with 5+ years of >15% returns), "52-Week High Stocks with Strong Fundamentals", "Mutual Fund Buying Momentum" (stocks where institutional buying is increasing). Each screen comes with rationale and is updated daily.
5
Set up Earnings Calendar → Know when your watchlist companies declare results
Go to Trendlyne → Calendar → Earnings. Filter by your watchlist. See result dates 2–4 weeks in advance. Check historical earnings surprises for each company — a company that beats estimates 8 out of 10 quarters is likely to keep doing so because of conservative guidance culture. Surprises to the upside usually cause 5–15% stock moves on result day.

For each watchlist stock, set two alerts: (1) Upper limit — price you'd consider selling at or above. (2) Lower limit — price where you'd consider buying more. App: Settings → Create Alert → Select stock → Set price threshold → Save

Track promoter/institutional shareholding → See who's buying and selling
Go to any stock page → Shareholding → Quarter-on-quarter changes. Increasing FII holding = foreign institutional investors buying. Increasing DII holding = domestic mutual funds accumulating. Decreasing promoter holding (especially with pledge) = warning sign. When multiple large institutions increase holdings simultaneously, it often precedes significant price movements over 3–6 months.

The Master Research Rule
Use All Three Together — Each Catches What the Others Miss
No single tool gives you the complete picture. Screener.in shows you the numbers — 10 years of financial truth — but tells you nothing about why those numbers are what they are, or what analysts expect. Trendlyne shows you quality scores and analyst consensus — but doesn't explain the underlying business model. Moneycontrol's management interviews explain the WHY — but without Screener's numbers, you can't tell if management's narrative matches financial reality.

The winning combination: Screener confirms financial quality → Trendlyne validates with analyst consensus and technical momentum → Moneycontrol provides the qualitative business thesis. All three pointing in the same direction = the highest-confidence research foundation available to a retail investor. Start every new stock idea by opening all three tabs simultaneously. In one evening of research, you'll know more about a company than most short-term traders who've traded it for years.
