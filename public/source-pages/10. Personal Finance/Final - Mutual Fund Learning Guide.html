What is a Fund of Funds?
A fund that invests in other mutual funds — not directly in stocks or bonds.
Instead of picking individual securities, a FoF manager selects a basket of best-in-class mutual funds. You get multi-fund diversification through a single SIP — one folio, one NAV, automatic rebalancing.

⚡ Why Every Investor Should Know About FoFs
🌐
Only Way to Access Foreign Markets via SIP
RBI limits direct overseas investment. International FoFs are the ONLY compliant route for Indian investors to buy US tech (Apple, Google, Amazon) via monthly SIP in rupees — no Demat, no foreign account.
💱
Natural Rupee Depreciation Hedge
INR depreciates ~3–4% vs USD annually. Your international FoF gains that currency movement as extra return. A 10% US market gain + 4% rupee fall = ~14% INR return automatically.
🎯
Instant Portfolio in a Single Click
Multi-asset FoF = equity + debt + gold in one NAV, one SIP, one folio. No rebalancing. No multiple fund tracking. Ideal for broad diversification with minimum complexity.
🥇
Recommended 5–15% Portfolio Allocation
Financial planners recommend 5–15% in international FoFs. This small allocation significantly reduces India-specific economic risk without giving up growth potential.
🧾
FoF Taxation Note: Most FoFs (including international and gold FoFs) are taxed as debt funds — gains taxed at your income slab rate regardless of holding period (post April 2023 rules). Exception: FoFs with 35%+ equity in underlying Indian equity funds may qualify for equity taxation (LTCG 12.5% after 1 year). Always check the fund's tax treatment. The Nippon Multi Asset FoF is currently treated as a debt fund for taxation despite its equity-heavy allocation.

Phase 1 — Build the Foundation (Year 1–2)
1
Build an Emergency Fund in a Liquid MF
Before investing a single rupee in equity, park 6 months of living expenses in a liquid fund (SBI/HDFC Liquid Fund). These earn ~7.2% vs 3.5% in savings accounts, and can be redeemed in just 1 day. This is your financial shock absorber — it prevents you from panic-selling equity during a job loss or medical emergency. Target amount: 6 × monthly expenses. Keep it in Liquid Fund, not savings account.
📌 Suggested funds: SBI Liquid Fund, HDFC Liquid Fund, Parag Parikh Liquid Fund
2
Get Adequate Life and Health Insurance
Buy term life insurance equal to 10–15x your annual income from a reputed insurer (HDFC Life, ICICI Prudential). Get a family health floater of at least ₹10 lakh. Without insurance, one emergency can destroy years of investments in a single day. Never mix insurance with investment — avoid ULIPs and endowment plans completely. Pure term + separate MF investment is always superior in total returns and coverage.
📌 Action: Compare term plans on PolicyBazaar. Choose 99%+ CSR insurers. Set and forget.
3
Maximise Tax Savings with ELSS SIP
Invest ₹1.5 lakh per year (₹12,500/month) in an ELSS fund to claim Section 80C deduction. Save up to ₹46,800 in taxes annually while simultaneously building equity wealth at 12–14% CAGR. The 3-year lock-in per installment is a feature, not a bug — it prevents panic selling during market crashes. Use SIP not lumpsum — rupee cost averaging works best for ELSS too. After 4 years of SIP, you have rolling monthly maturities available each month.
📌 Best picks: Quant ELSS, Mirae Asset ELSS, SBI Long Term Equity Fund
4
Start a SIP in a Nifty 50 Index Fund
Launch a monthly SIP of ₹2,000–₹5,000 in a Nifty 50 Index Fund. Expense ratio is just 0.18–0.20%. You match the Indian economy's long-term growth with zero fund-manager risk. Research shows index funds beat 80% of actively managed large-cap funds over 10+ years due to the compounding cost advantage. Boring is good — the best investing strategy is often the one you stick with for 20 years.
📌 Best picks: UTI Nifty 50 Index Fund (₹500 min), HDFC Nifty 50 Index Fund (₹100 min)
Phase 2 — Accelerate Growth (Year 3–7)
5
Add Mid & Small Cap for Higher Growth
Allocate 20–30% of your total SIP to mid cap funds and 10–20% to small cap. These generate significantly higher long-term returns (3–6% more CAGR vs large cap) but are volatile short-term — only invest money you won't need for 7+ years. Increase your total SIP by 10% every year (Step-Up SIP). Even adding ₹1,000/year to your SIP amount compounds dramatically — see the 4x corpus difference in the SIP Calculator tab.
📌 Mid cap: HDFC Mid-Cap Opportunities, Kotak Emerging Equity. Small cap: Nippon India Small Cap, SBI Small Cap
6
Add International Diversification (10–15%)
Invest 10–15% of portfolio in international funds. This hedges against INR depreciation — if the rupee falls 5% vs USD, your international fund gains 5% just from currency movement alone. Parag Parikh Flexi Cap holds Google, Meta, and Amazon alongside Indian stocks. Motilal Oswal Nasdaq 100 FoF gives pure US tech exposure. Geographic diversification means an India-specific economic downturn does not wipe out your entire portfolio.
📌 Suggested: Parag Parikh Flexi Cap Fund, Motilal Oswal Nasdaq 100 FoF, Mirae Asset NYSE FANG+ ETF FoF
7
Review and Rebalance Once a Year
If equity markets rally, your 70:30 equity-debt mix might become 80:20. Rebalance by adding more to the underweight category (typically debt/gold), not by selling equity — selling triggers capital gains tax unnecessarily. Annual review prevents both emotional decisions during market highs and missed opportunities during lows. Set a calendar reminder for January every year. Check: fund performance vs benchmark (3Y rolling), fund manager changes, and your goal timelines.
📌 Rebalance tool: Use CAS statement from CAMS or MF Central to see full portfolio in one view
8
Harvest LTCG Tax Benefits Every Year
Equity LTCG up to ₹1.25 lakh per year is completely tax-free. Every February/March, book gains up to this limit and immediately reinvest — this resets your cost basis at current higher NAV. Over 10 years of disciplined tax harvesting, you can save ₹5–15 lakhs in future tax depending on portfolio size. This strategy costs you nothing (you reinvest immediately) but systematically reduces your future LTCG tax liability. Always use Direct Plans to maximise the benefit.
📌 Action: Every March, check unrealised LTCG in your portfolio. Redeem up to ₹1.25L gain. Reinvest next day.
Phase 3 — Preserve & Harvest Wealth (Year 8+)
9
Shift to Balanced/Hybrid Funds Near Goals
3–5 years before your goal (retirement, child education, home purchase), gradually shift equity gains into Balanced Advantage Funds. These dynamically move between equity and debt based on market valuations — automatically protecting capital as you approach the goal without requiring emotional market-timing decisions. The shift should be gradual: 20% shift year 1, 40% year 2, 60% year 3, 80% year 4, 100% year 5. This smooths out the final outcome against unfortunate market timing.
📌 Best picks: HDFC BAF, ICICI Pru BAF, Edelweiss BAF — best for pre-goal capital protection
10
Use SWP for Tax-Efficient Retirement Income
Instead of redeeming your entire corpus as a lump sum, set up a Systematic Withdrawal Plan (SWP) for ₹50,000–₹1,00,000/month. Only the gains portion is taxed (at 12.5% LTCG after 1 year), not the principal — making it far more tax-efficient than FD interest which is 100% taxable at your slab rate. The remaining corpus continues to compound at 10–12% annually, meaning a ₹1 Crore corpus at 5% annual withdrawal rate sustains income for 30+ years while growing. This is the difference between running out of money and leaving wealth.
📌 SWP corpus: Park in HDFC BAF or ICICI Pru BAF. Withdraw 4–5% of corpus annually. Review every 3 years.
