Rule of 72 — Complete Guide

The most powerful mental math trick in investing

⚡

The Rule of 72

Divide 72 by your annual interest rate to get the approximate number of years it takes to double your money. It works for any investment — FD, mutual fund, real estate, or even inflation eroding your savings.

𝑓
Years to Double = 72 ÷ Annual Rate (%)
𝑓
Rate Required = 72 ÷ Target Years
🔬

Why 72? The Math Behind It

The exact formula for doubling time is ln(2)/ln(1+r) ≈ 0.693/r. Multiplying by 100 gives 69.3/rate%. The number 72 is used because it's divisible by many small numbers (2,3,4,6,8,9,12) — making mental math easy. For rates 6–10%, the error is less than 1%.

📉

Rule of 72 for Inflation

The rule works in reverse for inflation — it shows how quickly inflation halves your purchasing power. At 6% inflation: 72÷6 = 12 years for your money to lose half its value. This is why investing beats hoarding cash!

▸
72 ÷ 6% inflation = 12yr
✓
₹1L today = ₹50K value in 12yr
🔢

Rule of 70 vs Rule of 72

  • Rule of 72: Most widely used, best for 6–10% rates
  • Rule of 70: Slightly more accurate for continuous compounding
  • Rule of 69.3: Mathematically exact (ln 2 × 100)
  • For practical investing decisions: Rule of 72 is always accurate enough
🧮

Compound Doubling — The 8th Wonder of the World

What makes the Rule of 72 so powerful is understanding how multiple doublings multiply wealth. At 12% CAGR (doubling every 6 years): ₹1 lakh → ₹2L (6yr) → ₹4L (12yr) → ₹8L (18yr) → ₹16L (24yr) → ₹32L (30yr). Start investing early — every 6-year delay costs you 50% of your final wealth!

Rule of 72 Insights

How to use this rule to transform your investment thinking

⭐ Most Powerful
⏰

Early Start = More Doublings = 10x More Wealth

Starting at 25 vs 35 gives 10 extra years at 12% CAGR. That's nearly 2 extra doubling cycles. Your corpus at 60 is 4x more if you start at 25 vs 35. Time is your most powerful asset.

💡 25yr start: 5 doublings. 35yr start: 3 doublings. That's 4× less wealth!
📊 Strategy
📉

Use Rule of 72 for Inflation Planning

At 6% inflation, your ₹1 lakh loses half its purchasing power in 12 years. If you hold cash or keep money in savings account (4%), you're barely keeping up. This is why equity investment (12%+) is essential.

💡 Cash at 0% → 72÷0 = ∞ years to double. Inflation already halves it!
🎯 Goal
🎯

Reverse Rule: Target Doubling Time → Required Rate

Want to double money in 5 years? Need 72÷5 = 14.4% annual return. Want to do it in 4 years? Need 18% CAGR. This tells you which asset class is realistic for your timeline.

💡 5yr doubling needs 14.4% — achievable via small/mid-cap equity SIP
🔢 Math
⚡

The Cost of Fees in Doubling Time

1% extra fund expense ratio might seem small, but it's devastating over time. At 15% gross returns: 15% net → doubles in 4.8yr. 14% net (1% expense) → doubles in 5.1yr. Over 30 years, you lose nearly one full doubling cycle.

💡 Choose Direct Mutual Funds — save 0.5–1% expense ratio, gain one full doubling!
💡 Insight
🏦

FD Doubles in 10 Years. Inflation Halves in 12.

At 7.5% FD rate, money doubles in ~10 years. But at 6% inflation, purchasing power halves in 12 years. FD barely beats inflation in nominal terms. After tax (30% bracket), FD at 5.25% post-tax barely keeps pace.

💡 FD: Real doubling takes 20+ years after inflation. Equity: 6–8 years.
🔄 Compare
💎

Compare Any Two Investments in Seconds

Rule of 72 lets you compare investments without any calculator. "This fund gives 15% vs that one at 12%." → 15%: doubles in 4.8yr. 12%: doubles in 6yr. Over 24 years: 15% = 5 doublings (32x). 12% = 4 doublings (16x). 2× more wealth!

💡 3% rate difference = 2× more wealth over 24 years. Huge compounding effect.
