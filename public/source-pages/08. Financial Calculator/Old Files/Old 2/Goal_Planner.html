Goal Planning Guide

Complete roadmap to plan and achieve every financial goal

📝
The 5-Step Goal Framework
Proven process for every financial goal
🎯

Step 1: Define Precisely

Be specific: "Buy 2BHK in Pune for ₹1.2 Cr in 2035" beats "Save for home." Write the goal, exact amount in today's value, and hard deadline.

🔥

Step 2: Inflate the Target

₹1 crore today is not ₹1 crore in 15 years. At 6% inflation you need ₹2.4 crore. Always plan future value — this calculator does it automatically.

📊

Step 3: Pick the Right Asset

Short goals (<3yr): Debt funds, RD, FD. Medium (3–7yr): Balanced/hybrid funds. Long (>7yr): Pure equity index funds for maximum growth power.

⇧

Step 4: Start Step-Up SIP

Begin with what you can afford, increase 10% every year. Step-up SIP needs 30–40% lower starting amount than flat SIP for the same target corpus.

📅

Step 5: Annual Review

Every January: check progress, step up your SIP, rebalance portfolio, update target for revised inflation. Goals are living, breathing documents.

🛡

Protect the Goal

Buy term insurance (10–15× income). Without insurance, a family emergency can erase years of goal savings. Goal protection = non-negotiable.

🏠
Goal-Specific Planning Tips
Tailored advice for India's most common financial goals
🏠

Home Buying

Target 20–30% down payment — avoid 90% loan. Build a separate SIP for down payment. Consider property appreciation (5–7%/yr). Keep EMI under 40% of take-home pay.

🎓

Child Education

Education inflates 8–10%/year. Start from birth — 18 years of compounding is enormous. Use Sukanya Samriddhi (girls), ELSS SIP (tax-saving), or child plan mutual funds.

🌴

Retirement

You need 25× annual expenses as corpus (4% withdrawal rule). Add NPS for additional tax-free growth. At retirement, shift 60% to bonds. Never stop SIP after 50.

Why a step-up SIP creates dramatically more wealth with same starting commitment

💡 Key Insight: A 10% step-up SIP starting at ₹10,000/month builds 2.14× more wealth than a flat ₹10K SIP over 20 years — yet your first year payment is identical!

Goal Planning Pro Tips

Expert strategies to hit every financial goal you set

🏆 Top Tip
🎯

One Goal = One SIP Account

Never mix goals in a single mutual fund. Create separate SIP portfolios for each goal (home, education, retirement). Mixing goals leads to confusion, premature withdrawals, and goal failure. Clear separation creates emotional discipline.

Name your SIP literally — "Riya's IIM Fund" or "Pune Flat 2031"
💡 Smart Move
⇧

Auto-Increase SIP on Appraisal Month

Most platforms allow step-up SIP setup once. Schedule your annual SIP increase on your appraisal month. A 10% step-up on ₹10K/month for 20 years creates ₹2.14 crore vs ₹99.9 lakh for a flat SIP — same first year.

10% step-up = 2.14× more corpus for same starting SIP
⚡ Critical
🔒

Your Goal Fund is Untouchable

Never redeem a goal SIP for vacations, gadgets, or short-term needs. The true cost of a ₹50K redemption 10 years before goal maturity is ₹1.55 lakh (at 12%). Set up an emergency fund first — goal fund is locked.

₹50K withdrawn 10yr early = ₹1.55 lakh lost from corpus
