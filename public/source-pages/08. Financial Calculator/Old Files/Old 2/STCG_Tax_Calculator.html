STCG Guide

Understanding short-term capital gains tax and how to minimise it

📉

What is STCG?

Short-term capital gains arise from selling equity assets held for under 12 months or property under 24 months. Budget 2024 raised equity STCG to 20%, making it more expensive to trade frequently.

💡

Convert STCG to LTCG

Simply holding equity for 12 complete months converts taxable income from 20% STCG to 12.5% LTCG. On ₹10L gains, that is ₹75,000 saved — just by waiting a few more weeks.

📋

STCG Tax Formula

Equity STCG
𝑓
STCG = Selling Price – Purchase Price
𝑓
Tax = STCG × 20%
▸
+ 4% Health & Ed Cess
✓
Total = Tax × 1.04
🔄

Tax Loss Harvesting

Before March 31 each year, sell loss-making equity positions to realize STCL. This offsets STCG (and LTCG) from profitable trades. Reinvest in similar assets immediately or the same asset after 30 days.
