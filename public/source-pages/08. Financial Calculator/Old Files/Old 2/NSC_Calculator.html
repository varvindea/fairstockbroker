NSC Guide

Complete guide to National Savings Certificate — taxation, benefits, and comparison

📮

What is NSC?

National Savings Certificate is a post office savings instrument backed by the Indian government. Fixed 5-year tenure at 7.7% p.a. Interest is compounded annually but only paid at maturity. Highly safe (sovereign guarantee).

🏷️

Tax Treatment

  • Investment: 80C deduction (Old Regime, up to ₹1.5L)
  • Accrued Interest: Taxable at slab rate each year
  • Re-investment: Accrued interest deemed re-invested — 80C eligible in years 1-4
  • Maturity: Only final year interest is taxable
💰

NSC Formula

Maturity Calculation
𝑓
Maturity = Principal × (1 + Rate)^5
✓
At 7.7%: ₹1,00,000 × (1.077)^5 = ₹1,44,903
✓
Total interest: ₹44,903 over 5 years
🆚

NSC vs Other 80C Options

  • vs FD: NSC usually higher rate + 80C benefit
  • vs PPF: PPF is EEE (fully tax-free); NSC interest is taxable
  • vs ELSS: ELSS has market risk but higher potential returns
  • Best for: Conservative investors wanting safe 80C + fixed returns
