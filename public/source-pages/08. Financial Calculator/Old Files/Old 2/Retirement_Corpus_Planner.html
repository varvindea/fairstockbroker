The 5-Phase Retirement Timeline
What to focus on at each life stage
Age 20–30
🌱 Foundation Phase
Start ₹3,000/month SIP — even small amounts compound dramatically over 35 years. Get term + health insurance. Build emergency fund of 6 months expenses.
Age 30–45
🚀 Accumulation Phase
Aggressively build wealth with 80–100% equity. Increase SIP with every salary hike. Start NPS Tier 1 for ₹50K extra 80CCD(1B) tax saving. Clear all high-interest loans.
Age 45–55
⚖️ Consolidation Phase
Reduce equity to 60–70%. Increase debt allocation. Clear home loan. Max EPF, PPF, NPS. Review corpus — are you on track? Increase SIP aggressively if behind.
Age 55–60
🎯 Pre-Retirement Phase
Shift to 40% equity, 60% debt. Plan withdrawal strategy. Explore SCSS, PMVVY for guaranteed post-retirement income. Consider annuity for 30–40% of corpus.
Age 60+
🏖️ Retirement Phase
SWP from balanced/debt funds at 4% annual rate. Keep 2 years expenses in liquid. Bucket strategy for sequence-of-returns risk protection.

The Corpus Formula

𝑓
n = retirement duration years
⚡

Power of Starting Early

  • ₹5K/mo from age 25 → ₹3.2 Cr at 60
  • ₹5K/mo from age 35 → ₹1.1 Cr at 60
  • ₹5K/mo from age 45 → ₹30L at 60
  • 10-year delay = 65% less corpus
  • Start small, start NOW
🏦

Best Instruments by Phase

  • Age 25–45: Equity index SIP, NPS Equity Tier
  • Age 45–55: Balanced advantage, PPF
  • Age 55–60: Debt funds, SCSS, FD laddering
  • Post 60: SWP + SCSS + annuity portion
🎯

5 Mistakes to Avoid

  • Relying only on EPF and NPS (insufficient)
  • Underestimating inflation (6–7% in India)
  • Ignoring healthcare inflation (12%+)
  • Stopping SIP during market crashes
  • Withdrawing retirement savings for other goals

Pro Tips — Retirement Planning

Strategies that can add crores to your retirement corpus

🏆 #1 Most Powerful
⏰

The 10% Step-Up Rule Creates 2–3× More Wealth

Increasing your SIP by just 10% annually compounds dramatically. ₹10,000/month flat for 30yr @ 12% = ₹3.5 Cr. Same ₹10,000 with 10% annual step-up = ₹8.2 Cr — 2.3× more wealth for barely any extra effort each year.

₹10K flat → ₹3.5 Cr · ₹10K with 10% step-up → ₹8.2 Cr · Automate the increase on your SIP platform
💡 Tax Smart
💰

NPS Tier 1 Saves ₹15,000 Extra Tax Every Year

Beyond the ₹1.5L 80C limit, NPS gives an additional ₹50,000 deduction under 80CCD(1B). At 30% bracket this saves ₹15,600 in tax every year — invest those savings back into equity SIP for compounding.

₹15,000/yr tax saved → reinvested in equity for 30yr @ 12% = ₹52 Lakh extra at retirement
📊 Asset Allocation
⚖️

100 Minus Age — The Simplest Rule That Works

Equity allocation % = 100 − your age. At 30: 70% equity, 30% debt. At 50: 50/50. At 65: 35% equity, 65% debt. This automatic de-risking protects your corpus as you approach and enter retirement.

Rebalance once a year. Never rebalance during crashes — let equity recover first
