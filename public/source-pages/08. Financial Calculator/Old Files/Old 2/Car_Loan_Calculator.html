Car Loan Guide

Buy smart, finance smarter

🔢

Car Loan EMI Formula

𝑓
EMI = P × r × (1+r)^n / ((1+r)^n-1)
✓
₹8L@10.5%·5yr = ₹17,178/mo
✓
₹8L@10.5%·3yr = ₹25,946/mo
📊

New vs Used Car Loan

  • New: 10–12%, 90% LTV, 7yr max, easier approval
  • Used: 14–18%, 70–80% LTV, 5yr max, age restrictions
  • Used car must be ≤10yr old for most banks
📉

Car Depreciation Schedule

  • Year 1: 15–25% value loss (worst year)
  • Year 2–5: 10–15% per year
  • Year 5+: 5–8% per year
  • ₹12L car → ₹5–6L after 5 years
🎯

50/30/20 Car Budget Rule

Car total cost (EMI × tenure + insurance × years + maintenance) should not exceed 50% of annual take-home salary. Many Indians violate this and end up financially stressed.

Car Loan Pro Tips

Buy smart, save on interest

⭐ Key Rule
💰

40% Down Payment = Financial Freedom

Making 40% down payment on a ₹12L car means loan of ₹7.2L. Your EMI drops from ₹25,680 to ₹15,408/mo (5yr). You save ₹2.8L in interest AND own more equity from day 1.

💡 Higher down payment = less financial risk when car depreciates
⏳ Tenure
📅

Choose 3yr Tenure When Possible

3yr car loan costs ₹1.1L interest on ₹8L; 7yr costs ₹3.7L — 3.4× more! Cars depreciate fast. Shorter tenure means you own a debt-free asset while it's still valuable.

💡 3yr vs 5yr: Save ₹1.2L interest on ₹8L loan
🔍 Rate
🏦

Get Pre-Approved Before Visiting Showroom

Walk into a showroom with bank pre-approval in hand. This eliminates the dealer's ability to inflate financing costs. You know your rate, they know you're serious.

💡 Pre-approval takes 24hr — saves potentially ₹50K–₹1L in rate difference
📊 Total Cost
🧮

Calculate Total Ownership Cost, Not Just EMI

EMI is just one part. Factor: EMI × tenure + insurance (5yr) + maintenance + fuel. Total 5yr cost for a ₹12L car is ₹18–22L — nearly double the sticker price.

💡 Total cost of ₹12L car: ~₹20L over 5yr including all expenses
💳 Credit
⭐

750+ CIBIL = 0.5% Lower Rate = ₹1L Saved

Build credit score to 750+ before applying. The difference between 700 and 750 CIBIL can mean 0.5–1% lower rate — saving ₹25,000–₹50,000 on a ₹8L car loan over 5 years.

💡 Pay 12 months of bills on time → CIBIL 750+ guaranteed
🔄 Strategy
📆

Buy Car at Month/Quarter End

Dealers have monthly/quarterly sales targets. Last 3 days of the month, they offer better discounts and waive processing fees to meet targets. Negotiate harder at month-end.

💡 Month-end negotiation: ₹20K–₹50K discount possible + free accessories
