EMI Complete Guide

Everything you need to know before taking a loan

📐

EMI Formula Explained

Monthly EMI Formula
EMI = P × r × (1+r)^n / ((1+r)^n - 1) P = Principal | r = Monthly Rate = Annual Rate/12 n = Months = Years × 12
Example: ₹50L @ 8.5% for 20yr → r=0.708%, n=240 → EMI = ₹43,391
⚖️

Flat Rate vs Reducing Balance

  • Flat Rate: Interest charged on full principal throughout — misleading, higher cost
  • Reducing Balance: Interest on outstanding principal — used by all banks, fair
  • Flat 10% ≈ Reducing Balance 18% effectively!
  • Always confirm reducing balance rate with lender
📊

FOIR — Fixed Obligation to Income Ratio

Banks limit total EMI to 40–50% of take-home salary. If income = ₹80,000/mo, max total EMIs = ₹32,000–₹40,000. Existing EMIs reduce eligibility further. This is the most common reason for loan rejection.

🔑

Tenure vs Interest Trade-off

  • Longer tenure = lower EMI but far more total interest paid
  • ₹50L @ 8.5%: 10yr EMI = ₹61,993, total = ₹74.4L
  • ₹50L @ 8.5%: 20yr EMI = ₹43,391, total = ₹1.04Cr
  • Extra 10 years costs ₹29.6 lakhs more in interest!

🎯 Home Loan Tax Optimization
1

80C: Principal Repayment

Principal portion of EMI qualifies for ₹1.5L 80C deduction. Combine with PPF/ELSS to max out the limit strategically.

2

24B: Interest Deduction

Up to ₹2L interest on self-occupied property is deductible. For let-out property, full interest is deductible (no cap).

3

Joint Loan = Double Benefits

Co-borrowers each get full 80C + 24B benefits if both are co-owners. A couple can together save ₹3.12L in tax annually.

Loan Pro Tips

Borrow smart, save lakhs

⭐ Best Strategy
💰

Prepay Every Year — Even Small Amounts

Paying even 1 extra EMI per year on a 20yr loan cuts the tenure by 2–3 years and saves lakhs in interest. Use bonuses, tax refunds, or windfalls for part-prepayment.

💡 ₹50K extra/year on ₹50L @ 8.5% saves ₹7.2L in interest
📊 Rate Hunt
🔍

Negotiate Rate Below Repo Rate + 2.65%

RBI's repo rate is your anchor. Current repo = 6.5%. Best home loan = ~8.5%. If your bank charges 9.5%+, negotiate or switch — you're overpaying by lakhs.

💡 0.5% lower rate on ₹50L 20yr = ₹3.4L saved
💳 Credit
🎯

CIBIL 750+ = 0.5–1% Lower Rate

Build your credit score before applying. A 750+ CIBIL score qualifies you for the lowest rates. Pay all existing EMIs on time for 12 months before applying for a major loan.

💡 750 vs 700 CIBIL: ₹50L loan saves ₹6L over 20yr tenure
📅 Timing
📆

Start EMI on 1st of Month, Disburse 20th

Get disbursement close to month-end, start EMI from 1st of next month. This maximizes the free float period and reduces total interest slightly.

💡 Smart disbursement timing saves 1–2 EMIs over loan tenure
🏦 Type
⚡

Floating Rate > Fixed Rate for Long Loans

Fixed rates are 1–2% higher. Over 20 years, rates will fluctuate up and down. Floating rate borrowers benefit when RBI cuts rates. Fixed rates only make sense in rising rate scenarios.

💡 1% lower floating rate saves ₹6.8L over 20yr ₹50L loan
🔄 Balance
🔃

Balance Transfer if Rate > Current Rate by 0.5%

If your existing loan rate is 0.5% above current best rates, do a balance transfer. The one-time fees (0.5–1% of outstanding) are usually recovered within 18 months.

💡 Transfer ₹40L outstanding from 9.5% to 8.7% → saves ₹4.2L
