How Much Cover Do You Actually Need?
The HLV method vs thumb rule — and why they differ
🧮

HLV Method

Human Life Value calculates the present value of all future earnings. It's personalised — accounts for income, years, liabilities and existing assets. Most financial planners recommend this over simple multiples.

HLV Formula
Cover = (Income × Years) + Loans + Education − Assets
⚡

Quick Thumb Rule

10–15× annual income. Simple and fast but may under- or over-insure. Use as a sanity check after the HLV calculation — your HLV result should be at least 10× your income.

Example
₹12L income × 15 = ₹1.8 Cr minimum cover
🛡️

Term Insurance

Pure protection — highest cover at lowest premium. ₹500/month for ₹1 Cr at age 25. No investment component. Financial planners universally recommend: buy term, invest the rest.

  • Online plans 30% cheaper than offline
  • Add CI rider for critical illness
  • Choose CSR above 98% always
🏆

Essential Riders

  • Critical Illness: Lump sum on 36+ disease diagnosis
  • Accidental Death: 2× sum assured on accident death
  • Waiver of Premium: Cover continues if disabled
  • Terminal Illness: Accelerated payout on terminal diagnosis

Pro Tips — Term Insurance

Expert strategies to get best coverage at lowest cost

🏆 Most Important
⏰

Buy Early — Every Year Costs More

Term premium locks in at the age you buy. A 5-year delay from 25 to 30 increases your monthly premium by 30%. A 10-year delay from 25 to 35 increases it by 80%. You pay the higher premium for the full remaining term.

Buy at 25: ₹500/mo · Buy at 35: ₹900/mo · Difference over 30yr: ₹1.44 Lakh
💡 Smart Move
🚭

Quit Smoking → Save ₹350+/Month

Smokers pay 50–70% higher premium. After quitting for 12 months, most insurers reclassify you as non-smoker. Declaration + new medical = instant premium reduction on renewal.

₹350/mo savings × 30 years = ₹1.26 Lakh saved in premium alone
⚠️ Common Mistake
📋

Always Disclose Medical History

Never hide diabetes, hypertension, family history or past surgeries. If you do, the insurer can reject the claim citing non-disclosure — exactly when your family needs the money most.

Full disclosure = loaded premium; non-disclosure = claim rejection. Always choose full disclosure.
