FD Complete Guide

Everything about Fixed Deposits in India

🏦

What is an FD?

A Fixed Deposit (FD) is a financial instrument where you deposit a lump sum with a bank for a fixed period at a pre-agreed interest rate. Unlike savings accounts, FDs offer higher interest with guaranteed returns regardless of market conditions.

🔄

Compounding in FD

Most Indian banks compound interest quarterly. Monthly compounding gives slightly more returns. The formula: A = P × (1 + r/n)^(nt) where n = compounding frequency per year. Quarterly compounding slightly increases the effective annual rate.

A = P × (1 + r/n)^(n×t)
P=Principal, r=Rate, n=Freq, t=Years
🏅

Types of FDs in India

  • Regular FD: Standard deposit, fully taxable interest
  • Tax-Saver FD: 5yr lock-in, ₹1.5L deduction under 80C
  • Senior Citizen FD: Extra 0.25–0.5% rate benefit
  • Corporate FD: Higher rates, higher risk than bank FDs
  • NRE/NRO FD: For non-resident Indians, NRE is tax-free
⚡

FD vs Mutual Funds

  • FD Returns: 6.5–9.5% (guaranteed, fixed)
  • MF Returns: 10–15% (market-linked, not guaranteed)
  • FD Tax: Full interest taxed at slab rate
  • MF Tax: LTCG 12.5% only on gains (equity)
  • FD: Best for capital protection, near-term goals

Submit Form 15G / 15H

If your total income is below taxable limit, submit Form 15G (below 60) or 15H (senior citizen) at start of year — bank won't deduct TDS

If you need FD + tax saving, use Tax-Saver FD. ₹1.5L investment saves up to ₹46,800 in tax (30% bracket). 5-year lock-in applies.

FD Pro Tips

Maximize your Fixed Deposit returns

⭐ Golden Rule
🔗

FD Laddering Strategy

Instead of one 5-year FD, create multiple FDs maturing each year (1yr, 2yr, 3yr, 4yr, 5yr). This ensures annual liquidity and lets you reinvest at higher rates if interest rates rise.

💡 ₹5L split into 5 FDs of ₹1L each — one matures every year
📅 Timing
📊

Book FD at Year-End When Rates Peak

Banks often raise FD rates in Q4 (Jan–Mar) to meet deposit targets. Monitor rates and book during this window to lock in higher rates for longer tenures.

💡 Jan–March is historically the best time to book FDs in India
🏦 Safety
🛡️

Stay Within ₹5L DICGC Limit per Bank

DICGC insures only ₹5L per depositor per bank. For large amounts, spread across multiple banks to ensure full insurance coverage even if a bank fails.

💡 Keep < ₹5L per bank — use multiple banks for safety
📈 Returns
🎯

Small Finance Banks for Higher Rates

Small Finance Banks (Jana, AU, ESAF, Suryoday) offer 8.5–9.5% FD rates vs 7–7.5% from large banks. They're RBI-regulated and DICGC-covered up to ₹5L.

💡 Earn 1.5–2% more — just keep within ₹5L DICGC limit
💰 Tax
🧾

Submit 15G/15H Before April 1st

Submit Form 15G (below 60) or Form 15H (senior citizens) at the start of each financial year. This prevents TDS deduction if your total income is below taxable threshold.

💡 Submit by April 1 — otherwise TDS deducted for entire year
🔄 Smart
💡

Cumulative vs Non-Cumulative FD

Cumulative: Interest compounded and paid at maturity — ideal for wealth building. Non-cumulative: Monthly/quarterly interest payout — ideal for regular income retirees who don't need SWP.

💡 Building wealth? Choose cumulative. Need monthly income? Non-cumulative.
