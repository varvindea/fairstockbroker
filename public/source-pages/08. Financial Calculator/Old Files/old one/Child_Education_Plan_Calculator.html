Child Education Planning Guide

A systematic approach to building your child's education fund

🎓

Why Start at Birth?

Compounding rewards patience. Starting at birth vs age 5 means ₹8,500/mo instead of ₹15,000/mo for the same goal at 12% return. 5 extra years of compounding halves the monthly requirement. Every year of delay costs dearly.

📊

Best Instruments

  • Equity Index Fund SIP: Best for 10yr+ horizon (12–15%)
  • SSY: For girl child — 8.2% tax-free, government-backed
  • PPF: Safe component with EEE tax status
  • Avoid: Child insurance plans — high charges, low returns
💰

How Much to Save

Future Corpus Formula
Future Cost = Today's Cost × (1.10)^Years For ₹25L today, 15 years: ₹25L × (1.10)^15 = ₹1.04 Cr needed SIP required @ 12%: ~₹20,000/mo
🛡️

Protect the Goal

  • Buy term insurance equal to education target
  • Waiver of premium rider — SIP continues if disabled
  • Keep education fund separate from other savings
  • Review every 2–3 years and step up SIP
  • Diversify: equity SIP + SSY + partial debt
