Step-Up SWP Guide Book

Everything you need to know about Systematic Withdrawal Plans

❓
What is a Step-Up SWP?
The retirement income strategy that keeps pace with inflation
💸

What is SWP?

A Systematic Withdrawal Plan (SWP) lets you withdraw a fixed amount from your mutual fund corpus every month. Unlike FD interest, it's tax-efficient — only the gains portion is taxed, not the principal. Your remaining corpus continues to grow at market returns.

⬆️

What is Step-Up SWP?

A Step-Up SWP automatically increases your monthly withdrawal by a fixed percentage every year — typically 5–7% to match inflation. In Year 1 you withdraw ₹20,000/month, in Year 2 ₹21,000, Year 3 ₹22,050 — maintaining your real purchasing power throughout retirement.

🔢

The SWP Formula

Monthly Corpus Depletion
C(n) = C(n-1) × (1+r/12) – W(n)
Where: C = Corpus · r = Annual return · W = Withdrawal · W grows by step-up% each year. Computed month-by-month.
🏆

SWP vs FD Interest

  • SWP: Only gains taxed (12.5% LTCG after 1yr) — far more efficient
  • FD: Entire interest taxed at slab rate (up to 30%)
  • SWP: Principal + gains can be withdrawn
  • SWP: Corpus continues to grow tax-efficiently
  • FD: Fixed rate, no market upside participation
🛡️ The 4% Safe Withdrawal Rule
A global retirement planning benchmark — adapted for Indian investors
📏
The Rule
Withdraw no more than 4% of your corpus in Year 1. Increase by inflation annually. Historically, this lasts 30+ years.
🇮🇳
India Adjustment
With 6–7% inflation vs 2–3% in US, India's safe rate is ~3.5%. Build in buffer. Target 25–30× your annual expenses as corpus.
📈
Keep Equity
Maintain 40–60% equity even in retirement. This keeps returns above withdrawal rate so corpus lasts longer.
🔄
Annual Review
Review your SWP amount each year. If markets gave 15%, increase withdrawal. If -10%, pause the step-up for that year.

💡 SWP Tax Advantage — Why it beats FD
1

FIFO — Old Units Redeemed First

When you start SWP, the oldest units (held longest) are redeemed first. If your SIP was running for 3+ years, almost all SWP redemptions will qualify as LTCG (12.5%), not STCG.

2

Principal is Tax-Free

Only the profit (gain) portion of each SWP instalment is taxed. If you invested ₹50L and it grew to ₹80L, only the ₹30L gain is taxable — spread across many years of SWP.

3

Harvest ₹1.25L LTCG Exemption Yearly

Structure your SWP so annual gains redeemed stay under ₹1.25L/year — then you pay ZERO LTCG tax. For larger SWPs, book profits in April each year to utilise the exemption.

Step-Up SWP Pro Tips

Expert strategies to make your corpus last a lifetime

🏆 Most Important
📏

Match Step-Up to Inflation, Not Salary

Unlike SIP (where you step up by salary hike %), SWP step-up should match India's CPI inflation of 5–7%. Going higher erodes corpus faster; going lower reduces your real purchasing power over time. Start at 5–6% and review annually.

Rule: SWP Step-Up % ≈ India CPI inflation (5–7%). Never exceed your expected fund return rate.
💡 Corpus Longevity
🛡️

Bucket Strategy — Split Your Corpus

Divide corpus into 3 buckets: Bucket 1 (2yr expenses in liquid funds — don't touch during markets), Bucket 2 (5yr in hybrid funds), Bucket 3 (rest in equity). This ensures you never sell equity in a bear market to fund withdrawals.

Bucket 1: Liquid/Overnight · Bucket 2: Hybrid · Bucket 3: Large Cap/Index
⚡ Tax Strategy
🧾

Pause SWP During Market Crashes

The biggest risk to SWP is Sequence of Returns Risk — withdrawing during a bear market permanently destroys corpus. When markets fall 20%+, switch SWP from equity to Bucket 1 (liquid fund) for 3–6 months. Resume when markets recover.

Keep 2 years of expenses in liquid funds. Never sell equity during a crash.
📊 Structuring
📅

Optimal SWP Date: 5th of the Month

Set SWP date between 1st and 7th. Avoid month-end — NAV can be volatile near dividend/bonus dates. The 5th gives enough time for the credit to reflect in your bank account by 8th–10th. Set autopay on the 1st for bill payments after SWP credit.

SWP date: 1st–7th → Credit arrives: 3rd–10th → Bill payments: 10th+
🔥 Advanced
⚖️

Rebalance Annually — Not Just SWP Amount

Every April, check your equity:debt ratio. If equity rallied 30%, your 50:50 ratio may be 65:35 now. Book equity profits (tax-efficient up to ₹1.25L), move to debt, rebalance back. This "buy low, sell high" automated discipline compounds your corpus significantly.

April ritual: Tax-loss harvest + rebalance + SWP review. One hour/year saves lakhs.
⏳ Long Term
💰

Never Draw Down 100% — Leave 30% as Legacy

Plan your SWP to exhaust only 70% of corpus over your retirement horizon. The remaining 30% acts as: (a) buffer if you live longer than expected, (b) healthcare emergency fund in later years, (c) legacy for family. Add term insurance if corpus is below ₹50L.

Target: 70% corpus utilized · 30% legacy/buffer at end of planning horizon
