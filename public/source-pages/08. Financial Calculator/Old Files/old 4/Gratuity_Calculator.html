Gratuity Guide

Complete guide to gratuity rules, eligibility, and tax treatment

🏭

What is Gratuity?

Gratuity is a statutory retirement benefit under the Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972. It rewards employees for long service — payable on retirement, resignation (after 5yrs), or death/disablement. Calculated on Basic + DA salary.

🔢

Gratuity Formula

Payment of Gratuity Act
𝑓
Gratuity = (Basic + DA) × 15 ÷ 26 × Years
💡
Example: ₹80,000 × 15 ÷ 26 × 25 = ₹11,53,846
▸
Tax-Free: ₹11,53,846 (within ₹20L limit)
🏷️

Tax on Gratuity

  • Government employees: Fully exempt, no limit
  • Private sector: Up to ₹20L tax-free (Sec 10(10))
  • Amount above ₹20L: Taxable as salary
  • No TDS on gratuity below ₹20L
💡

Maximising Your Gratuity

  • Complete full years — partial counted if ≥6 months
  • Negotiate higher basic (vs allowances) in CTC
  • Push for promotion before retirement
  • File nomination form (Form F) with employer
  • Know your rights under the Act
