STP — Best of Both Worlds

Systematic Transfer Plan: Park lumpsum in liquid, transfer monthly to equity

Park in Liquid/Overnight Fund
Earns ~6.5-7% while waiting

📅

Auto-transfer fixed amount

→
📈

Receives monthly transfer
Full equity exposure gradually
🏆 STP Advantage
  • Earn 6.5-7% on undeployed amount (vs 0% in savings account)
  • Rupee cost averaging like SIP — buy more units in market dips
  • Reduce timing risk of lumpsum market entry
  • Best strategy when markets appear expensive (P/E > 22)

Pro SIP vs Lumpsum Tips

Expert strategies for every market condition

🏆 Key Insight
📊

SIP Wins in Volatile Markets

Rupee-cost averaging means SIP buys more units when prices fall. During the 2020 COVID crash, SIP investors bought Nifty units at 7,500 while lumpsum investors who entered at 12,000 waited 9 months to recover.

2020 COVID: Nifty fell 38% in 30 days. SIP investors recovered 12 months before lumpsum investors
💡 Smart
💰

Bonus Season = Lumpsum Opportunity

Received a bonus, incentive, or matured FD? Deploy as lumpsum if Nifty P/E is below 20. If P/E is above 22, use STP: park in liquid fund, transfer ₹50K-1L monthly over 12 months.

Jan-Feb (advance tax refund), March (FY-end bonuses) — check P/E before deploying
⚡ Never Do This
🚫

Never Stop SIP During Crash

Market crashes feel terrifying but they are SIP's best friend. Each rupee buys more units at lower NAV. The 2008, 2020 crashes recovered fully within 2-3 years. Stopping SIP locks in losses.

Sensex crashed 52% in 2008 → recovered 100% by 2010. SIP investors who continued made the most
📈 Advanced
🔄

Monthly STP: The Perfect Hybrid

Have a large bonus? Don't choose between SIP and lumpsum. Use STP: park full amount in a liquid fund (earning ~7%), then auto-transfer ₹1-2 lakh monthly to your equity fund over 6-12 months.

STP benefit: Earn 7% on waiting money + rupee-cost average into equity = Best of both worlds
