RD Complete Guide

Everything you need to know about Recurring Deposits

🪙

What is an RD?

A Recurring Deposit (RD) is a special savings account where you deposit a fixed amount every month for a fixed period. At maturity, you receive the total deposited amount plus compound interest. It's like an EMI — but for savings instead of loans.

📐

RD Formula

Indian banks use quarterly compounding for RD interest calculation.

𝑓
M = R × [(1+i)^n - 1] / (1-(1+i)^(-1/3))
𝑓
R=Monthly deposit, i=Rate/4, n=Quarters

Each monthly deposit starts compounding from its deposit date.

⚖️

RD vs FD vs SIP

  • RD: Monthly deposit, guaranteed returns, fully taxable
  • FD: Lumpsum, guaranteed returns, fully taxable
  • SIP: Monthly investment, market returns (10–15%), tax-efficient
  • For wealth building (5yr+): SIP > RD significantly
  • For capital safety: RD/FD guaranteed; SIP is market-linked
✅

When RD Makes Sense

  • Short-term goals (1–3 years) with capital protection need
  • Building emergency fund systematically each month
  • Low-risk investors who need guaranteed returns
  • Discipline tool: Auto-debit enforces monthly saving habit
  • Better than letting money sit in savings account (3–4%)

Each bank's ₹40K TDS threshold applies separately. Multiple banks = less TDS likelihood per bank

RD Pro Tips

Get more from your recurring deposits

⭐ Golden Rule
🪙

RD for Safety, SIP for Wealth

Use RD for short-term goals (1–3yr) and capital preservation. Switch to SIP for goals beyond 3 years where equity returns (10–15%) will significantly outperform RD (7–8%).

💡 ₹10K/mo for 10yr: RD gives ₹17.6L, SIP @12% gives ₹23.2L
📅 Strategy
🔗

Stack Multiple RDs for Liquidity

Instead of one 5yr RD, open 5 different RDs with 1yr, 2yr, 3yr, 4yr, 5yr tenures. One matures every year, giving annual liquidity. Reinvest in higher-rate options each year.

💡 RD laddering = liquidity every year without breaking any deposit
🏦 Rate
⬆️

Small Finance Banks for More Returns

Small Finance Banks (AU, Jana, ESAF) offer 8–9% RD rates vs 7–7.5% from big banks. They're RBI-regulated and DICGC-insured up to ₹5L — perfectly safe for amounts within the limit.

💡 Extra 1.5% on ₹10K/mo for 5yr = ₹34,000 more at maturity
📱 Tech
📲

Enable Auto-Debit — Never Miss a Month

Missing even one RD installment may attract penalties and break the compounding chain. Set ECS/NACH auto-debit from salary account on 5th or 10th of each month — day after salary credit.

💡 Auto-debit removes the temptation to spend before saving
💰 Tax
🧾

RD Interest is Taxed Every Year — Not at Maturity

Many RD investors think tax is paid only at maturity. Wrong — RD interest is taxable each year as it accrues. Factor this into your net return calculation. Actual post-tax return can be 20–30% less.

💡 At 30% slab: 7.5% RD → effective 5.25% post-tax return
🎓 Education
🏫

Best For: Specific Short-Term Goals

RD is perfect for building a down payment, vacation fund, or gadget purchase in 1–3 years. The mandatory monthly deposit creates savings discipline. Combine with a liquid fund for emergencies.

💡 Short-term specific goal? RD. Long-term wealth building? SIP.
