Annuity Buying Guide 2026

Choose the right plan for guaranteed lifetime income

Plan Type
⚡
Immediate vs Deferred Annuity
Immediate annuity pays within 1 month of purchase — ideal for retirees needing income now. Deferred annuity accumulates at a guaranteed rate for a set period, then pays out — use it if you're pre-retirement and want to lock in today's rates before RBI cuts push them lower.

1–30yrDeferral Options

Spouse Cover
💑
Single vs Joint Life Annuity
Joint life annuity continues paying your spouse after your death at 50–100% of original (depending on plan). Monthly income is 10–15% lower than single life but provides essential longevity protection. Under IRDAI 2025 regulations, joint life plans must now offer at least 50% continuation for spouse.

Timing Strategy
🎂
Why Buying at 65+ Gets You 30% More
Annuity rates improve significantly with age — insurer expects fewer payout years. A 65-year-old gets 25–35% more monthly income than a 55-year-old for the same corpus. Strategy: use SWP from 60–65 for better tax efficiency, then annuitise at 65+ for guaranteed lifetime income at premium rates.

NPS Rules
🏛️
NPS Annuity — How It Works in 2026
At NPS maturity (60+), minimum 40% of corpus must buy annuity from IRDAI-empanelled Annuity Service Providers. The remaining 60% is tax-free lumpsum. 11 ASPs available including LIC, SBI Life, HDFC Life, ICICI Pru. NPS annuity income is taxable at your applicable slab rate under both Old and New regimes.

Breakeven Maths
🧮
Breakeven Age — Know Your Number
Breakeven Age = Purchase Age + (Corpus ÷ Annual Payout). Example: buy at 65, ₹50L corpus, 6.4% rate = ₹3.2L/year. Breakeven = 65 + 15.6 = ~81 years. India life expectancy: 70–73 years for men. Statistically, many buyers won't reach breakeven — but annuity is longevity insurance, not investment.

Tax Treatment
💸
Annuity Tax vs MF SWP — Big Difference
Annuity income is 100% taxable as 'Income from Other Sources' at slab rate. MF SWP: only the gain portion taxed at 12.5% LTCG. On ₹1Cr corpus in 30% bracket: annuity annual tax ≈ ₹1.9L vs SWP annual tax ≈ ₹0.25L — a ₹1.65L annual saving from SWP, every year for life.

Annuity — Smart Buying Tips 2026

Maximise your guaranteed pension income

01
Never Annuitise 100% of Your Corpus
Convert only 40–60% to annuity for guaranteed income. Keep the rest in SWP or balanced funds. This hybrid approach gives guaranteed base income (annuity) + flexible inflation-adjusted income (SWP) + capital for emergencies. 100% annuity locks in low fixed income with no flexibility ever after.
🎯 Hybrid Approach
02
Always Compare at Least 3 Insurers
LIC, SBI Life, HDFC, ICICI Pru, Max Life — rates differ by 10–15% for the same corpus. ₹1Cr at LIC (6.4%) = ₹53,333/mo vs ICICI Pru (5.9%) = ₹49,167/mo — ₹4,166/mo more from LIC over your lifetime. Use PolicyBazaar or Coverfox to get all quotes simultaneously.
💰 Rate Shopping
03
Choose Return-of-Purchase-Price Option
ROP returns your full purchase price to nominees after death. Monthly income is 15–20% lower than non-ROP, but your capital is preserved for heirs. Choose ROP if you have dependents or want to leave an inheritance. If no dependents and need maximum income, choose without ROP.
🏆 Capital Preserved
04
Lock In During High Interest Rate Cycles
Annuity rates track RBI repo rate and bond yields. With RBI keeping rates elevated in 2025–26, current annuity rates (6.4–6.8%) are near multi-year highs. If you're planning to retire in 1–2 years, consider locking in now with a deferred annuity rather than waiting for possibly lower rates.
📈 Rate Lock Strategy
05
Port to 60-Day Window Under IRDAI 2025
Under IRDAI 2025 regulations (effective April 2025), the porting window for annuities has been clarified — compare ASPs carefully at NPS withdrawal time. You cannot switch annuity providers after purchase for immediate annuities. Spend at least 2–3 weeks comparing before deciding.
⚖️ Due Diligence
06
Consider Inflation-Indexed Annuity for 30yr Horizon
Some insurers offer annuities increasing 3–5% annually. Income starts 20% lower but grows every year — preserving purchasing power over a 30-year retirement. By Year 18, an inflation-linked annuity surpasses regular annuity in cumulative receipts. Essential if retiring at 55–60 with a long horizon ahead.
🌱 Inflation-Proof
