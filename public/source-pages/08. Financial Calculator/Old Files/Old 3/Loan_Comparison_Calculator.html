Loan Comparison Guide

How to choose the right loan and lender for your situation

💰

Total Cost vs EMI

A longer tenure means lower EMI but higher total interest. A 20-year ₹50L loan at 9% costs ₹59.7L in interest. At 15 years, EMI is higher but total interest is only ₹41.5L. Choose tenure based on your cash flow, not just monthly affordability.

🔄

Balance Transfer Savings

If your existing home loan rate is 9.5% and SBI offers 8.75%, a balance transfer saves ₹6.4L on ₹50L over 15 years. Balance transfer costs ~1% processing fee + legal charges. Break-even is typically reached in 12–18 months of lower EMI.

📊

FOIR — Your Borrowing Limit

Banks use Fixed Obligation to Income Ratio (FOIR). If your income is ₹1L/month and existing EMIs are ₹25K, you can add only ₹15K more EMI (40% FOIR = ₹40K max, minus ₹25K existing). Understanding this helps you know exactly how much loan you qualify for.

📈

Floating vs Fixed Rate

Floating rate loans (linked to repo rate) currently offer 8.5–9%. Fixed rate loans are 1–2% higher but predictable. If you expect rates to fall (RBI cutting repo rate), floating is better. In a rising rate environment, lock in fixed rates quickly.

⚡

Prepayment Strategy

Prepaying a home loan of ₹50L at 9% with ₹1L extra annually in years 1–5 saves ₹14.3L in interest and cuts tenure by 5.7 years. PSU banks have zero prepayment charges on floating rate home loans — take advantage of every bonus or windfall.

🏆

Credit Score Impact

Your CIBIL score determines the rate you get. Score above 750 = best rate (8.5%). Score 700–750 = +0.25%. Score below 700 = +0.5–1%. One point of rate difference on ₹50L/20yr = ₹6–8L extra cost. Build your CIBIL before applying.

Loan Comparison Tips

How to negotiate and pick the cheapest loan every time

🧮

Compare APR, Not Just Rate

Some banks advertise a low interest rate but have high processing fees (1–2%), legal fees, and insurance charges. Always compare Annual Percentage Rate (APR) which includes all fees. A 8.5% loan with 2% processing = 8.9% effective APR.

💡 APR = (Total cost incl. fees / Loan amount) / Years × 100
📉

Negotiate After Pre-Approval

Get pre-approval from 3 banks. Then use the best offer to negotiate with your preferred bank. Home loan rates can be negotiated down by 0.25–0.5% simply by showing competing offers. On ₹50L/20yr, 0.25% lower rate = ₹3.2L saved.

📊 Always apply to PSU bank (SBI/BoB) + 1 private bank for comparison
🔄

Refinance When Rates Drop

When RBI cuts repo rate, check if your bank passes on the cut. If not within 3 months, use the RBI directive on home loan rate resets and demand reduction. If they refuse, apply for balance transfer — break-even is typically under 18 months.
