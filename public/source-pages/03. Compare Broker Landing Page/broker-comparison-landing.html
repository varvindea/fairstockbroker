What We Compare

13 Key Parameters That Matter

From per-order costs to financial stability — every metric you need to pick the right broker for your trading style.

💸
Brokerage Charges

Per-order fee for Delivery, Intraday, F&O, Commodity and Currency. The single biggest cost difference across brokers.

⭐ Most Critical
🏦
Account Opening Fee

One-time cost to open a demat + trading account. Ranges from ₹0 (most discount brokers) to ₹999 (HDFC Securities).

💡 Often Free
📅
AMC Charges

Annual Maintenance Charge for your demat account with CDSL/NSDL. Ranges from ₹0 lifetime (mStock) to ₹750/yr (HDFC).

📆 Yearly Cost
💳
DP Charges

Depository Participant charge per sell transaction. Ranges from ₹9 (Shoonya) to ₹35 (ICICI Direct). Adds up for active traders.

📦 Per Sell
📱
Trading Platform

Quality of the mobile app, web platform, desktop software, charting tools and execution speed and reliability.

🖥️ UX Matters
📊
Margin & Leverage

Intraday and overnight leverage available for equity, F&O and commodity segments. Higher = more risk & opportunity.

⚠️ Risk Factor
🔗
Segments Available

NSE, BSE, MCX commodity, CDS currency, Mutual Funds, US Stocks, IPO, SGB & NPS access across the broker's platform.

🌐 Coverage
🎧
Customer Support

Phone, chat, email availability and resolution speed. Critical when trade execution fails during market hours.

📞 24×7 or Not?
🔬
Research & Advisory

In-house equity research, analyst calls, screeners, portfolio advisory and wealth management. Full-service brokers lead here.

📑 Full-Service Edge
🤖
Technology & API

Algo trading API quality, TradingView integration, Smallcase support, uptime reliability and charting depth for power users.

⚙️ For Algo Traders
🌍
NRI & Global Access

NRI PIS account, US stock investing, international fund access and NRE/NRO account compatibility for diaspora investors.

✈️ For NRIs
👥
Active Client Base

NSE-verified active client count as of Feb 2026. A larger base signals trust, infrastructure investment and regulatory stability.

📈 Trust Signal
🏛️
Financial Stability

Backing strength, parent group credibility, years in operation, profitability, SEBI compliance record and client fund safety.
