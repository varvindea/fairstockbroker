Pros & Cons

An honest assessment of where Motilal Oswal excels and where it falls short compared to discount brokers in 2026.

✅
Advantages
  • ✓India's Best Equity Research: The annual Wealth Creation Study (30+ editions) and 250+ stock coverage by SEBI-registered analysts — this in-depth, independent research is MO's biggest competitive moat over discount brokers.
  • ✓Flat ₹20 Brokerage (No Subscription): The Flat Fee Plan offers Zerodha-level pricing with no monthly subscription — active traders get full-service research at discount broker cost.
  • ✓India's #1 PMS Provider: Consistently ranked India's top Portfolio Management Service by returns and AUM — HNI clients get access to focused equity portfolios backed by 38 years of research.
  • ✓Listed Company — Full Transparency: MOFSL is listed on both BSE (532892) and NSE (MOTILALOFS) — quarterly results, annual reports, and regulatory filings are fully public. Rare among Indian brokers.
  • ✓Comprehensive MO Ecosystem: Broking, MO AMC (₹1.2L Cr+ AUM), PMS, AIF, home finance, investment banking — all under one listed group with a proven 38-year track record.
  • ✓2,500+ Business Locations: Extensive pan-India branch and franchise network — among the largest physical presence of any full-service broker, ensuring local support across Tier 2 & 3 cities.
  • ✓NRI & Corporate Accounts: Full NRI account support via PIS route, corporate and HUF accounts with dedicated RMs — serving all investor types at ₹0 account opening.
⚠️
Limitations
  • ✗Percentage Plan is Expensive: The default Percentage Plan at 0.20% delivery is more expensive than Zerodha (₹0 delivery), Groww (₹0), or Angel One. New accounts must manually switch to Flat Fee Plan.
  • ✗Higher Demat AMC than Discount Brokers: ₹300/yr (1st Year Free) Demat AMC — higher than Zerodha's ₹300/yr and significantly higher than Groww's ₹0 lifetime, though better than HDFC Securities' ₹750/yr.
  • ✗No NPS or Fixed Deposits: National Pension Scheme (NPS) and Fixed Deposits are not available on the MO platform — investors need separate accounts, unlike ICICI Securities which offers both.
  • ✗No Joint Demat Accounts: MO does not support joint demat or trading accounts — limiting household joint investment management. ICICI Securities and Kotak Securities offer joint demat options.
  • ✗App Fragmentation: Two separate apps (MO Trader for trading, MO Investor for wealth) creates a split experience — unlike Zerodha Kite which handles everything in one app with a unified interface.
  • ✗No Native No-Code Algo Platform: No built-in strategy automation like Zerodha Streak — retail traders need third-party tools or technical API skills for systematic trading at MO.

Flat Fee Plan vs Percentage Plan — which one should I choose?+
For almost all investors, the Flat Fee Plan (₹20/order) is significantly better. The Percentage Plan only makes mathematical sense for investors who place very small orders (under ₹4,000) very rarely. Here is how to decide:
Break-Even Analysis
  • 1Flat Fee Plan (₹20 — Best for Most): ₹20 is cheaper when your delivery trade value exceeds ₹4,000 (₹4,000 × 0.50% = ₹20). Since most investors trade above ₹4,000 per order, the Flat Fee Plan wins. Also: no subscription fee required.
  • 2Percentage Plan (0.20% Delivery — Only for Micro Orders): The only scenario where % Plan is cheaper is if your average delivery trade is consistently below ₹4,000 in value. Almost no serious investor operates at this level.
  • ✓F&O Traders: Always use Flat Fee Plan — ₹20 flat for all futures and options orders is identical to Zerodha, beating the 0.05%/1% % plan significantly on all but the smallest orders.
  • ✓How to switch: Log in to MO Trader app → Menu → Account Settings → Brokerage Plan → Select Flat Fee. Takes effect from next trading session.

What account types does Motilal Oswal NOT support?+
Motilal Oswal does not support two specific account types — Joint Demat Accounts and Direct Foreign National/OCI accounts (without a PIS-enabled Indian bank account). All other standard account types are fully supported at ₹0 opening fee.
Not Supported
  • !Joint Demat Account — Not Available: MO does not offer joint demat or joint trading accounts. SEBI permits these, but MO's structure does not support them. Consider ICICI Securities or Kotak Securities for joint demat. Each investor must open a separate individual account at MO.
  • !Foreign National/OCI without PIS Bank Account — Not Available: Foreign nationals, OCI holders, and PIOs who do not have a PIS-enabled NRE/NRO bank account with an Indian bank cannot open an MO account directly. The PIS approval must be obtained from an authorised Indian bank first.

Can NRIs open a Motilal Oswal account? What are the restrictions?+
Yes — Motilal Oswal supports NRI demat and trading accounts via the RBI's Portfolio Investment Scheme (PIS) route. NRIs need a PIS-enabled NRE or NRO account at an authorised Indian bank before applying. The process is offline and takes 10–15 days.
  • ★NRE Account (Repatriable): Allows equity delivery and mutual fund investments only — profits are fully repatriable to your home country. F&O, currency, and commodity trading are NOT permitted on NRE accounts.
  • ✓NRO Account (Non-Repatriable): Allows equity delivery, mutual funds, and F&O trading — profits have limited repatriability. Currency and commodity trading not permitted under FEMA regulations for NRIs.
  • ✓Free account opening: ₹0 account opening fee for NRI accounts. Demat AMC ₹400/yr + 18% GST applies (same as resident accounts).
  • !PIS mandatory: You must have PIS (Portfolio Investment Scheme) approval from your Indian bank before MO can open your trading account. Authorised PIS banks include SBI, HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, Axis Bank, and others.
  • !Offline process only: NRI account opening is not available online — contact MO's NRI helpdesk at [email protected] or 1800-102-9090 for a dedicated NRI RM.
✓ NRE: Equity + MF (Repatriable) ✓ NRO: Equity + MF + F&O ⚠️ No Currency/Commodity (FEMA) ⚠️ PIS Bank Account Required First
🌐 For NRIs looking for the most seamless onboarding, HDFC Securities (via HDFC Bank NRI cell) or ICICI Securities (via ICICI NRI banking) may offer a more integrated experience. MO is ideal for NRIs who already bank with an NRE/NRO PIS-enabled Indian bank.

Does Motilal Oswal support algo trading or API access?+
Motilal Oswal supports algorithmic trading via its MO API (trading API) for technical users and authorised persons. However, it does not offer a built-in no-code algo platform like Zerodha Streak for retail traders. Third-party platform integrations are also available.
  • ✓MO Trading API: REST API access for algorithmic and systematic trading — supports order placement, position tracking, live market data, and portfolio management. Available for authorised persons and technical traders.
  • ✓Third-party integrations: MO supports integration with platforms like Sensibull (options analytics), Smallcase (thematic investing), and select SEBI-registered algo platforms via API bridge.
  • ✓ProTerminal: MO's professional desktop terminal supports advanced order types and API connectivity for institutional and semi-systematic traders.
  • !No native no-code algo builder: Zerodha Streak, Angel One SmartAPI, and Upstox have built no-code strategy testing and auto-execution tools for retail traders. MO does not have an equivalent retail-friendly algo platform.
  • !Technical skills required for API: MO API requires Python/programming knowledge or use of authorised API vendors. This is not beginner-friendly for non-technical traders.
✓ REST API Available ✓ Sensibull + Smallcase Supported ⚠️ No Built-in No-Code Algo Technical Skills Required
